# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release.

- `isValidNuban`, `validateNuban`, `computeCheckDigit`, `buildNuban`, `findMatchingBankCodes` and `toInstitutionCode`
- Support for 3-digit bank codes and 5-digit OFI codes, following the CBN Revised NUBAN Standards (2020)
- ESM and CommonJS builds with TypeScript types
